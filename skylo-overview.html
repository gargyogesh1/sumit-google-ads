The Philosophy Behind SKYLO by Central Park

SKYLO by Central Park represents a new benchmark in ultra-luxury residential living in South Gurugram (Sohna). Conceived for the discerning few who seek natural tranquility without compromising on metropolitan connectivity, SKYLO pairs awe-inspiring panoramic vistas of the age-old Aravalli hills with manicured golf greens.

Central Park has long stood as a pioneer of hospitality-driven residential developments across Delhi NCR. Following celebrated landmarks such as Central Park Resorts, Flower Valley, and The Room, SKYLO is designed with high-volume ceiling heights, wide private wrap-around sundecks, and state-of-the-art climate control systems.
