About Developer

Central Park is one of the top names in India’s real estate. With a proven track record of delivering world-class residential and commercial projects, the developer has become synonymous with quality, timely delivery and lifestyle innovation. Projects like Central Park Resorts, Central Park Flower Valley and Central Park Sky Villas have set benchmarks in premium living. Central Park’s philosophy is to go beyond building homes – it creates experiences, communities and lifestyle-driven spaces.
