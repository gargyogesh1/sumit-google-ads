SKYLO by Central Park – South Gurugram

SKYLO by Central Park is an iconic new luxury residential launch situated in South Gurugram (Sohna). Framed by the timeless natural majesty of the Aravalli hills and tranquil golf greens, SKYLO introduces expansive studio apartments residences starting at ₹3.30 Cr*.

Crafted with signature Central Park craftsmanship, residents enjoy high ceiling clearances, private viewing decks, five-star hospitality services, and seamless signal-free transit to Rajiv Chowk and Golf Course Extension Road via the Sohna elevated corridor.
