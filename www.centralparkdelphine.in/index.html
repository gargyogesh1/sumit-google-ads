Overview

Central Park Delphine, Sector 104 Gurgaon

Central Park Delphine is a landmark residential project in Sector 104 Gurgaon that blends luxury, comfort, and exclusivity. Spread over 8 acres of green land, Central Park 104 offers a mix of studio & 1 BHK apartments - 2 Towers, 3 BHK & 4 BHK apartments - 4 Towers. With world-class design, premium architecture and amenities crafted for modern living, Central Park 104 has emerged as one of the most sought-after residential destinations on the Dwarka Expressway belt. Its sky walk innovation, high-rise towers and thoughtfully designed layouts make it stand out in the luxury housing segment.

All About Central Park Delphine – Sector 104 Gurgaon

Central Park Delphine is the new upcoming high rise residential project — providing luxury living on Dwarka Expressway Gurgaon. Every detail, from the grand entrance lobbies to the sky walk cafe overlooking the skyline, has been crafted to redefine urban sophistication. The residences offer 13 ft. ceiling heights, giving every home a sense of openness, grandeur and architectural brilliance rarely seen in the NCR region. The Sky Walk, a themed lifestyle space spread over an acre at the tower-top, is one of the most distinctive features. Imagine sipping coffee in a sky-high cafe, walking amidst curated landscapes at elevated levels and enjoying city views like never before — that’s the promise Delphine 104. Inside your residence, luxury is expressed through expansive layouts, premium finishes, and thoughtfully designed interiors. Be it the sprawling 5500 sq. ft. 4 BHK or the compact-yet-premium 940 sq. ft. studio, every unit is a masterpiece of design and comfort. Beyond homes, the luxury extends to a world-class clubhouse, complete with fitness studios, lounges, pools, and wellness zones. Security, concierge services, and smart home features ensure that residents experience not just luxury, but also peace of mind and convenience.
